# Science Result Harvest | evidence only (existing Skills 05–07)

**Purpose:** preserve scientific value that may be hidden in completed experiments, and describe a real algorithm contribution correctly without writing a paper. Research priority and Q2/Q1 publication potential remain owned by PD-RW. This is not a new Skill, state, permission, evaluator, journal score or manuscript authorization.

## When

Only after a meaningful VALID DEV Run, material negative mechanism result, a new verified comparator, or before expensive escalation, project closure or the research-results summary. Reuse the existing Task/Run/selection history and prior Harvest when nothing material changed; do not revisit every seed.

## Find overlooked scientific evidence

Inspect already permitted matched predictions/metrics and negative Runs (with source receipts), independent group support, condition/position/device/time limits, label-calibration/compute costs, model errors and rival explanations. Choose 1–2 scientifically motivated axes, not a post-hoc sweep for the largest number. Results from a selected subgroup are exploratory; SHAP attribution is not causal explanation; a no-gain mechanism may still constrain a hypothesis. Archive the relevant full scope and adverse results.

Keep up to 3 *distinct* scientific Claim candidates with:
`CLAIM_TYPE | IMPORTANT_KNOWLEDGE | NEAREST_ORIGINAL_GAP | POSITIVE_AND_NEGATIVE_RUN_REFS | GROUP_UNIT+SELECTION_HISTORY | STRONGEST_ALTERNATIVE | DISTINCT_PREDICTION | EXPLORATORY/CONFIRMED | MOST_DECISIVE_EVIDENCE+AUTH/COST`.
PD-RW/user chooses the most valuable science Claim; the Forge's cheapest experiment-action ordering must never select the research question.

## Distinguish scientific articulation from missing scientific proof

The non-manuscript `CONTRIBUTION_EVIDENCE_BRIEF` records:
`IMPORTANT_PROBLEM | ACTUAL_NEW_KNOWLEDGE_OR_CAPABILITY | NAMED_NEAREST_ORIGINAL+GAP | FAIR_RUN_EVIDENCE+ADVERSE_FINDINGS | STRONGEST_ALTERNATIVE | VALID_SCOPE/INDEPENDENT_GROUPS | ARTICULATION_GAP/EVIDENCE_GAP/BOTH/NONE | NEXT_DECISIVE_ACTION+AUTH`.

- `ARTICULATION_GAP`: evidence supports the scoped scientific contribution but existing research summary omits why it matters, how it differs from named nearest work, or key evidence. Improve Claim–Evidence mapping or diagnostic evidence tables **without extra tuning or writing manuscript prose**.
- `EVIDENCE_GAP`: mechanism/generalization/causal/value claim lacks decisive controls or independent evidence. Keep it explicitly unproven; propose the cheapest legal discriminating experiment.
- `BOTH`: first fix evidence-level interpretation, then seek the decisive missing proof.
- `NONE`: reuse existing research evidence; no embellished story needed.

**No manuscript writing while `WRITING_LOCKED`**: the user's review of the research-results summary and a separate explicit manuscript-writing authorization are still mandatory. Do not output submission title, abstract, Results, Discussion, outline or full draft. Any figure (including a candidate manuscript figure) additionally requires an explicit user request to draw those specific images; figure permission does not authorize manuscript prose.
