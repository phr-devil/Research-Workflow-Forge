# 07 | Scientific confirmation

Shared rules: [EXECUTION_POLICY](../../docs/EXECUTION_POLICY.md); scientific confirmation is **not** manuscript-writing authorization. Enter only on a real candidate claim with eligible independent evaluation; it may be planned early but cannot run sealed resources without actual approval.

- Freeze claim, task, code/data/evaluator, checkpoint, legal inputs, metric, group independence and prior selection history before confirmation.
- Read full named nearest papers and compare the **actual scoped contribution**, not only method novelty, title or metadata overlap. One credible incremental Claim is sufficient to justify scientific confirmation; global SOTA, a brand-new architecture, all-positive metrics and multi-dataset dominance are not generic requirements. Select strongest *relevant* fair baselines, report all relevant adverse outcomes, group-level uncertainty, and only the ablation/cost/physical checks needed to substantiate that Claim. Efficiency and label-cost contributions must disclose predictive tradeoffs and actual resource measurement.
- Examine DATA_ACCESS and Task selection history: merely renaming a split or changing seeds cannot restore untouched holdout status. Preserve failed confirmations.
- Separate **DEV effect**, independent confirmation and genuine paper-level novelty. Structural passes, synthetic fixtures, Oracle, and model reviews do not prove scientific Claims.

**Small-upgrade scientific confirmation:** [POST_ALGORITHM_SCIENCE_AUDIT.md](../../docs/POST_ALGORITHM_SCIENCE_AUDIT.md) may supply a DEV-only group pattern or mechanism hypothesis; confirm only after freezing the specific Claim and receiving separate sealed-evaluation authorization. Post-hoc slices, mismatched K/info, attribution without controlled tests or reused contaminated holdouts cannot support new confirmed knowledge. Negative confirmation remains evidence, not a trigger to write a manuscript.

**Q2+ evidence assessment:** legitimate independent confirmation only establishes the frozen Claim's scientific evidence. Before declaring project research success, compare that Claim with real recent articles in a verified JCR Q1/Q2 target and its specific category/year, using the PD-RW scientific/venue policy. `Q2_POTENTIAL_SUPPORTED` needs defensible publication potential, **not acceptance**, and never grants manuscript-writing permission. An unconfirmed or metadata-only analogy stays `Q2_UNCERTAIN`.

**Contribution evidence sufficiency before final summary:** refer to [SCIENCE_RESULT_HARVEST.md](../../docs/SCIENCE_RESULT_HARVEST.md); make sure the strongest scoped scientific contribution was compared with overlooked evidence, meaningful negative results, closest original work and actual group/selection limits. If prior evidence already supports the Claim but is described poorly, update a non-manuscript `CONTRIBUTION_EVIDENCE_BRIEF`; if controlled evidence is missing, keep the Claim qualified and seek original user approval for new confirmations. Never strengthen a causal/physical explanation by better wording or begin Results/Discussion writing.

**Research-evidence / user-summary handoff (not permission to draft): summarize a single supported claim, primary evidence/result table, nearest published work and difference, strongest matched competitor, independent validation/uncertainty, adverse results, limits and decisive evidence still missing. Route only the **decisive** evidence gaps into authorized experiments. Before the user has reviewed a research-results summary and explicitly authorized writing, do **not** generate a paper outline or any manuscript section. A submit-ready recommendation requires claim-matched independent support, truthful disclosure and a verified relevant article type/venue. Do not demand a novel backbone or all-model leaderboards.

**Advisory journal handoff:** a credible small, Claim-proportional contribution can be a `PAPER_PATH_CANDIDATE` with explicit gaps even if not SOTA, but this does **not** establish Q2+ competitiveness or writing permission; do not confuse this with `CONFIRMED` or sealed evaluation. For optional manuscript-potential S/A/B/C and journal STRETCH/TARGET/FALLBACK, refer to [PD-RW venue strategy](https://github.com/phr-devil/Problem-Data-Research-Workflow/blob/main/VENUE_STRATEGY.md). They are human-facing recommendations, not Forge states, article acceptance probabilities or substitutes for full nearest-neighbor comparison.

**Human decision handoff:** when confirmation requires yet-ungranted holdout access, project-level success/STOP, research-summary review or manuscript-writing permission, provide recommendation and choices only; do not emit a sealed-evaluation or manuscript Agent prompt. Already-approved technical evidence checks may receive a concise next-Agent instruction. [NEXT_ACTION_POLICY](../../docs/NEXT_ACTION_POLICY.md).

**Handoff:** ClaimRecord pointing to actual source/Run receipts and limits, or INSUFFICIENT / CONFIRMATION_UNAVAILABLE. An eligible untouched within-dataset group may confirm a carefully restricted Claim; external datasets become necessary when the claim genuinely spans datasets/domains or the original confirmation resource is ineligible. Forge still does **not** execute independent scientific confirmation autonomously. Publication figures are never automatic: they require both eligible results and the user's explicit request to draw the specific image(s), independent of manuscript-writing authorization. See [Figure QA Contract](../../docs/FIGURE_QA_CONTRACT.md).

**v0.7:** SEALED Run ingestion through BASELINE_VALID/RUN_RESULT/NO_GAIN/RUN_INVALID is refused. This is fail-closed development bookkeeping, NOT a completed independent-confirmation service. No JSON authorization_ref alone can grant permission to inspect a sealed dataset. The full execution boundary must be separate from the Agent's DEV workspace.

**Narrow Claims:** multi-target/direction/dataset outcomes may support a restricted effect or credible boundary but cannot be repackaged as universal gain through post-hoc cherry-picking. Preserve entire search/selection history, negative targets and direction reversals; require eligible independent confirmation and named nearest-work comparison. Individually trained dataset-specific models are not evidence that weights transferred across platforms.
